# Brand Strategy — Format Reference

> **What this is.** The exact structure `/brand` writes to
> `intelligence/brand-strategy.md`. This is the **strategic** half of the
> brand: who the brand is, who it's for, and where it sits in the market. (The
> **visual** half — colour, type, photography, packaging, ad style, and the
> prompt modifier — lives in `visual-guidelines.md`, written in the same run.)
>
> Write clean markdown: `#` title, `##` for each section, `###` for each
> question, answers as prose or tight bullet lists, one blank line between
> blocks. Fill every field from the `/brand` research. Where information is
> genuinely missing, note the gap rather than guessing.

---

## Output structure

```
# [Brand Name] — Brand Strategy

## Brand Personality & Values

### What three words describe this brand's personality?
[Three precise words, plus one line on why each fits.]

### What does the brand stand for (and against)?
[What it champions, and the thing it actively rejects. The full counter-position
lives in counter-positioning.md — here, capture the values-level for/against.]

### How should people feel when they interact with the brand?
[The intended emotional response — 3–5 precise feelings, not generic adjectives.]

## Target Audience Deep Dive

### Who is the brand trying to reach?
[Be specific — psychographics, demographics, behaviors, beliefs, habits. Never
"everyone". Describe a person, not a segment.]

### What are their pain points, desires, and aspirations?
[The frustrations they carry, what they want, who they're trying to become.]

### Where do they spend their time, online and offline?
[Platforms, communities, physical places, media diet.]

### What other brands do they love, and why?
[Adjacent brands they already pay for, and the reason — this reveals the values
and aesthetics they respond to.]

## Competitive Landscape

### Who are the direct and indirect competitors?
[Name them. Direct = same category; indirect = different category solving the
same job.]

### What visual territories are oversaturated?
[The looks everyone in this space already uses — to be avoided.]

### Where is the white space to own?
[The visual / positional territory that is open, that this brand can credibly
take.]
```

---

## Notes

- Heading levels are load-bearing: `#` title, `##` the three sections, `###`
  each question. Keep it
  clean — proper line breaks, one idea per block, no walls of text.
- **This is NOT the brand-detection marker.** In The Creative Stack, the file
  that marks a folder under `brands/` as a real brand is
  `intelligence/visual-guidelines.md`. Always write that file too.
- `brand-strategy.md` is what downstream copy and concept work reads for *who*
  the brand is; `visual-guidelines.md` is what image skills read for *how* it
  looks; `brand-voice.md` is *how it writes*; `counter-positioning.md` is *why
  it wins*.
